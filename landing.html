The Complete Path

From written exam to final merit.

Most academies stop at the written test. We walk with you through every stage.

02

Daily ground training — 1.6 km run, pull-ups, ditch jump and stamina building. Timed practice sessions.

03

Medical Guidance

Pre-medical screening and prep so nothing surprises you at the military hospital. Vision, dental and general health.

04

Final Selection

Merit list preparation, document verification and post-selection guidance — all the way to your joining letter.
